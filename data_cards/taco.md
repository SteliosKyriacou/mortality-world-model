# Data card: TACO rodent transcriptomic meta-dataset

*Last verified: 2026-10-03.* Molecular arm (PLAN.md §3.3). Mouse and rat, bulk RNA-seq plus microarray,
mostly cross-sectional, with intervention labels and the authors' group-level lifespan and hazard estimates.

## Data source and access

- **Aggregated rodent meta-dataset: available.** Zenodo record **10.5281/zenodo.18763485**, *Transcriptomic
  clock models and rodent gene expression meta-dataset* (published 2026-02-25), ~64 GB in total, almost all of it
  Bayesian-ridge clock pickles that we don't need. Licence: **MGB Open Access License 1.0** (non-commercial
  academic research; no redistribution of derived products for commercial use). Open download, no account.
- New data generated by the paper: GEO SuperSeries **GSE292885** (GSE292843, GSE292884, GSE293503).
- The full accession list of the 96 rodent sources is in Nature Supplementary Table 1b (downloaded) and in the
  paper's Data availability statement. Sources by sample count:
  GSE132040 (752) · GSE53960 (304, rat) · GSE141252 (249) · PRJNA516151 (234, rat) · GSE190939 (230) ·
  GSE75574 (223) · GSE201207 (211) · PMID 34666007 (197) · Current/ITP (161) · GSE234563 (144) · GSE75192 (112) ·
  GSE146977 (98, rat) · GSE34378 (87) · GSE81959 (72) · GSE48333 (69) · GSE131754 (63) · GSE122116 (59) ·
  GSE145480 (50) · GSE139204 (47) · … 77 smaller studies (5–42 samples each). Rat sources: GSE53960,
  PRJNA516151, GSE146977, GSE27625, GSE66715.
  Because the aggregate exists, we **didn't** download per-study GEO count files.

## Contents

- **N = 4,539 samples**, 96 sources. Mouse: 3,876 (many strains: C57BL/6J, UM-HET3, BXD panel, dwarfs, etc.),
  rat: 663. Sex: 3,084 male, 1,305 female, 150 unknown. Platform: 3,566 RNA-seq, 973 microarray.
- **Tissues (26):** liver 1,503 · skeletal muscle 587 · brain 515 · kidney 300 · vWAT 243 · heart 236 · lung 196 ·
  spleen 120 · skin 112 · adrenal 92 · small intestine 84 · blood 74 · BAT 73 · scWAT 69 · bone 55 ·
  bone marrow 46 · thymus 44 · spinal cord 36 · aorta 34 · stomach 29 · esophagus 20 · prostate 18 ·
  large intestine 17 · uterus 16 · nerve 12 · bladder 8.
- **Ages:** mouse 4–930 days (median 364), rat 14–810 days (median 360). In months: 0.13–30.6.
- **Interventions (79 plus control):** control 3,575 samples. Diet 419 (CR variants, HFD, keto, low BCAA, Met
  restriction), genotype 266 (Ames/Snell/Little dwarfs, GHRKO, progeroid Lmna/Ercc1/BubR1/Klotho/Terc, nmrHas2 …),
  compound 245 (rapamycin, acarbose, 17α-estradiol, canagliflozin, metformin, ITP compounds …), combinations 31,
  exercise 3.
- **Lifespan labels (from the authors):** per-group expected 90th and 99.9th-percentile lifespan, expected
  log10 hazard at the sample's age, and differences vs matched controls. Missing for 52 samples (some Ercc1
  progeroid groups). We derive `lifespan_effect_log_ratio = log(p90 group / median p90 of controls in the same
  study × species × sex)`, which is 0 for controls and NaN for 26 samples with no matched estimate. It is
  negative for progeroid models (Klotho KO −2.2, Lmna progeria −1.8) and positive for CR or dwarfism (+0.2 to +0.3).
- **Features (important):** the values are **not log1p CPM**. Rows are **mouse Entrez IDs** (the authors mapped rat genes
  to mouse orthologs). "Scaled" = the authors' preprocessing: log expression **standardised per sample across genes** (each
  sample has mean ≈ 0 and sd ≈ 1–1.5 over its measured genes; liver Alb ≈ +3.7). Gene-level differences are kept,
  but **study, platform and batch effects are not removed**. NaN = gene not measured or filtered in that dataset. The
  "relative" matrix is centred per gene on each study's matched controls (the authors' input for the clocks), so it
  holds age, intervention and genotype effects without study offsets. We map
  mouse Entrez → human HGNC symbol with MGI **strict one-to-one** orthologs (17,178 mouse↔human pairs; a human gene claimed by several mouse paralogs is dropped): 18,592 mouse genes → 16,160 (relative matrix: 18,286 → 16,195) human genes.
  Missingness: 27.7% of cells are NaN. Genes measured in ≥ 50% / ≥ 90% / 100% of samples: 12,220 / 6,442 / 712.
  Re-deriving true log-CPM would require per-study GEO count files (≈ 96 downloads; microarray studies cannot
  give counts). That isn't done here; GSE132040 raw counts are available via `mwm.data.tms.load_bulk()`.
- **Time axis:** `age_frac_lifespan = age / species median lifespan` via `mwm.data.common.lifespan_fraction`
  (shared constants in `common.py`: mouse **26 months**, rat **30 months**). These are approximations: C57BL/6J
  median ≈ 26–30 months depending on sex and colony (e.g. Yuan et al., Aging Cell 8:277, 2009; ITP UM-HET3
  controls ≈ 26–29 months), and the F344 rat median ≈ 24–31 months (NIA colonies). The paper's own normalisations are
  kept too: `age_frac_maxlifespan_taco` (age / species max lifespan: mouse 1,460 d, rat 1,387 d) and `age_norm_taco`
  (age / group-specific 99.9th-percentile lifespan).

## Usage

```bash
python scripts/fetch_orthologs.py      # MGI homology -> data/raw/orthologs/
python scripts/fetch_taco.py           # ~1.85 GB (use --no-relative to skip 0.72 GB)
python -m mwm.data.taco                # (with PYTHONPATH=src) builds data/interim/taco/*.parquet
```
```python
from mwm.data import taco
X, meta = taco.load()                       # X (4539, 16160) float32 with NaN; meta (4539, 26)
X, meta = taco.load(min_coverage=0.9)       # X (4539, 6442)
R, meta = taco.load(relative=True)          # relative-to-control matrix
Xm, mm = taco.load(species="mouse")
```

## Caveats
- Microarray and RNA-seq are mixed. Filter with `meta.platform_type` if needed.
- Use study-level splits (PLAN.md §3.4). Study and batch effects remain in the absolute matrix, so don't read the study effect as biology (or use the relative matrix).
- Licence: non-commercial. Derived latents or weights inherit the MGB licence terms, so check them before publishing.
